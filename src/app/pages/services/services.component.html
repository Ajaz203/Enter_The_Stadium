RFID Cards & Tags

Types of RFID Cards

  • LF (Low Frequency) 125 kHz – Basic access
  • HF (High Frequency) 13.56 MHz – MIFARE®, iCLASS®
  • UHF (Ultra High Frequency) – Long-range applications
  • NFC-enabled cards – Smartphone interactions
  • Security ID cards – Holograms, UV, microtext
  • Combo cards – HF+LF, UHF+LF, UHF+HF
  • Specialty cards – Wooden, LED, Metal

RFID Tags

  • Passive: LF, HF, UHF, NFC
  • Specialty: LED, High Temp, Anti-metal, Flexible
  • Full-color artwork, holograms, UV ink, microtext for security
  • Custom form factors: key fobs, wristbands
