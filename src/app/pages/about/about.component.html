Types of RFID Cards

  • LF (Low Frequency) cards – 125 kHz for basic access
  • HF (High Frequency) cards – 13.56 MHz, including MIFARE®, iCLASS®
  • UHF (Ultra High Frequency) cards – Long-range applications
  • NFC-enabled cards – Smartphone interactions
  • Security ID cards – Holograms, UV, microtext
  • Combo cards – HF+LF, UHF+LF, UHF+HF
  • Specialty cards – Wooden, LED, Metal
  • Full-color secure printing with UV, holograms, microtext
  • Custom form factors: key fobs, wristbands

Types of RFID Tags

  • Passive RFID Tags – LF, HF, UHF, NFC
  • Specialty Tags – LED, High Temp, Anti-metal, Flexible
  • Full-color artwork printing with high resolution
  • Holograms, UV ink, and microtext for security
  • Secure encoding of UID and application data
  • Custom form factors: key fobs, wristbands
